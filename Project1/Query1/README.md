# Telecom Churn Intelligence

## Business Context
A mid-size telecom operator is losing 8% of its subscriber base per quarter.
The retention team needs a data-driven system to identify at-risk customers
BEFORE they churn, prioritise outreach by revenue impact, and measure
the effectiveness of past retention actions.

## Business Tasks
1. Identify customers showing early churn signals (30/60/90 day inactivity)
2. Rank at-risk customers by monthly revenue at stake
3. Detect customers on high-value plans with declining payment behaviour
4. Find regions where churn is clustering — network or service issue?
5. Build a reactivation candidate list: churned customers worth winning back

## Tools Used
MySQL 8.0 · CTEs · Window Functions · Subqueries · Views
