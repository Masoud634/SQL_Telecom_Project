Business Context:
High churn is rarely spread evenly across a network. It clusters.
When multiple subscribers in the same region stop paying and go quiet
at the same time, it usually means one of two things a service quality
problem, or a network infrastructure failure.
The difference matters enormously. A retention team calling churned
customers with loyalty offers won't fix a broken tower. This query
separates the two signals so the right team gets the right alert.


What the Query Does

Calculates the churn rate for every region (churned subscribers / total subscribers)
Computes the national average churn rate across all regions
Counts open support tickets per region broken down by network tickets and critical priority
Compares each region against the national average
Flags regions more than 50% above average as high risk
Combines churn signal + network ticket signal to produce a recommended action
